# Unit 5 Resources: Simulations & Case Studies 📚

## 📖 Recommended Textbooks
1.  **"Simulation Modeling and Analysis"** by Averill Law.
    *   *Why*: The authority on **Monte Carlo** simulations.
    *   *Chapters*: 1 (Basic Simulation Modeling), 2 (Simulation Software).
2.  **"Think Stats"** by Allen Downey.
    *   *Why*: Python-based **EDA** approach. Teaches you to code simulations instead of just doing math.
    *   *Chapters*: 5 (Modeling Distributions), 9 (Hypothesis Testing).
3.  **Specific Case Study Papers**:
    *   "Random Walk Hypothesis" (Stock Market).
    *   "Quality Control Charts" (For Resistor Case).
