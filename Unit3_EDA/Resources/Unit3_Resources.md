# Unit 3 Resources: Data Quality, Missing Data & Tools 📚

## 📖 Recommended Textbooks
1.  **"Python for Data Analysis"** by Wes McKinney.
    *   *Why*: The creator of pandas!
    *   *Chapters*: 4 (Pandas Basics), 5 (Getting Clean Data), 7 (Data Cleaning and Preparation).
2.  **"Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn"** by Aurelien Geron.
    *   *Why*: Excellent for understanding *when* to drop data and *when* to impute.
    *   *Chapter*: 2 (End-to-End Machine Learning Project).

## 📺 Video Tutorials (Free)
1.  **StatQuest with Josh Starmer** (YouTube)
    *   *Video*: "One-Hot Encoding vs. Label Encoding" (Categorical Data).
    *   *Video*: "Data Cleaning: Missing Values".
2.  **Corey Schafer - Python Pandas Series** (YouTube)
    *   *Video*: "Pandas: DataFrames - Dealing with Missing Data (dropna, fillna)"
    *   *Why*: Very hands-on, great for beginners.

## 📊 Datasets for Practice
1.  **Kaggle - House Prices: Advanced Regression Techniques**
    *   [Link](https://www.kaggle.com/c/house-prices-advanced-regression-techniques)
    *   *Practice*: Massive amount of *Missing Data* (PoolQC depends on "PoolArea" being > 0). Excellent for imputed logic.
2.  **UCI - Adult Income Dataset**
    *   [Link](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/datasets/adult)
    *   *Practice*: "Unknown" categories ("?"), skewed Income data.

## 📄 Key Articles & Guides
*   **"Missing Data Mechanisms" (MCAR, MAR, MNAR)**:
    *   [Link](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4121561/)
    *   *Summary*: The definitive theoretical explanation of why data is missing.
*   **"Cook's Distance" Guide**:
    *   [Link](https://statisticsbyjim.com/regression/cooks-distance/)
    *   *Summary*: Understand Leverage vs Influence visually.
