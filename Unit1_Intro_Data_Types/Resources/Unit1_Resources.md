# Unit 1 Resources: Data Types & Statistics 📚

## 📖 Recommended Textbooks
1.  **"Statistics for Business and Economics"** by Anderson, Sweeney, and Williams.
    *   *Why*: The gold standard for understanding Data Types, Scales (NOIR), and Sampling.
    *   *Chapters*: 1 (Data and Statistics), 2 (Descriptive Statistics).
2.  **"Head First Statistics"** by Dawn Griffiths.
    *   *Why*: Uses visual learning and distinct examples (like the "Fat Pencil" chart) to explain Histogram vs Bar Chart.
    *   *Focus*: Great for understanding *why* measuring scales matter.

## 📺 Video Tutorials (Free)
1.  **Khan Academy - Statistics and Probability**
    *   [Link](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability)
    *   *Topics*: Types of statistical studies, Sampling methods, Bias.
2.  **StatQuest with Josh Starmer** (YouTube)
    *   *Video*: "The Histogram, explained."
    *   *Video*: "Population vs Sample."
    *   *Style*: Extremely visual and simple. "Bam!"

## 📊 Datasets for Practice
1.  **UCI Machine Learning Repository - Iris Dataset**
    *   [Link](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/datasets/iris)
    *   *Practice*: Identify Nominal (Species) vs Ratio (Sepal Length) data.
2.  **Kaggle - Titanic: Machine Learning from Disaster**
    *   [Link](https://www.kaggle.com/c/titanic)
    *   *Practice*: Handle Mixed Data Types (Name vs Age vs Ticket Class).
