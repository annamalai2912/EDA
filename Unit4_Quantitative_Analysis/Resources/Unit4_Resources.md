# Unit 4 Resources: Quantitative Techniques 📚

## 📖 Recommended Textbooks
1.  **"Head First Statistics"** by Dawn Griffiths.
    *   *Why*: The **Best** explanation of Probability Distributions (Normal, Binomial, Poisson).
    *   *Chapters*: 4 (Probabilities), 8 (Normal), 9 (Sampling Dist).
2.  **"Practical Statistics for Data Scientists"** by Peter Bruce.
    *   *Why*: Cuts the math jargon and focuses on *when* to use tests (ANOVA vs t-test).
    *   *Chapters*: 2 (Data and Sampling Distributions), 3 (Statistical Experiments).

## 📺 Video Tutorials (Free)
1.  **StatQuest with Josh Starmer** (YouTube)
    *   *Video*: "The Normal Distribution, clearly explained!!!" (Essential).
    *   *Video*: "ANOVA" (Analysis of Variance).
    *   *Video*: "The F-Statistic, clearly explained!!!" (For ANOVA).
2.  **Khan Academy - Random Variables & Probability Distributions**
    *   [Link](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability/random-variables-stats-library)
    *   *Topics*: Binomial, Poisson, Geometric Distributions.

## 📊 Datasets for Practice
1.  **Student Performance Data Set** (UCI)
    *   [Link](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/datasets/student+performance)
    *   *Practice*: Run **ANOVA** on Test Scores (Math, Reading, Writing) across different Schools (Groups).
2.  **Wine Quality Dataset** (UCI)
    *   [Link](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/datasets/wine+quality)
    *   *Practice*: Check **Normality** of Alcohol content. Use **Bartlett's Test** for variance.
